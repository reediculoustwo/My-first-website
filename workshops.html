Workshop Options

We can tailor our visit to suit your department’s needs. Potential activities include:

  • Instrument Presentation: A presentation including a short performance.
  • Chamber Coaching: Sessions for chamber groups of any instrumentation.
  • Side-by-Side Session: An interactive experience where we play side-by-side within a chamber group, band and/or orchestra.
  • Masterclasses: An opportunity for students to get feedback from a different perspective.
  • Reed-Making Presentation: An overview of what a reed is, how it’s made, and tips for adjustment.
